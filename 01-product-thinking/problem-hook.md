# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic Velocity (B2B). The risk is concrete and close: our largest enterprise account is already trialing a leaner competitor, and the people who run fleets every day are using RouteLogic less.
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If we do nothing for 12 months, the company will lose the daily user while still winning the executive demo. Coordinator usage is already falling across the base, and our largest account is trialing a leaner rival because RouteLogic slows its most time-sensitive work. That trial is the first visible exit, not the only one. As coordination moves to phones, spreadsheets and lighter tools, RouteLogic becomes the back office admins log into for reports. A reporting layer on top of work done elsewhere is easy to replace. "The most powerful operating system" becomes "the expensive back office."
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** The user is currently forced to work around RouteLogic at the moments that matter most. A driver calls in late and a route has to be reassigned before the trucks leave. RouteLogic can do it, but the steps sit behind layers built for compliance and finance. So the coordinator solves it by phone, in a group text with drivers and on a spreadsheet, then types the result into RouteLogic later, if at all. The system of record drifts away from what actually happened on the road.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must keep our largest account and reverse the drop in daily use across our base by making coordinators' most time-sensitive work faster inside RouteLogic than on the phone and spreadsheet they fall back on today.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For frontline fleet coordinators, the 95% who rely on RouteLogic every day, we will make assign, reroute and track faster than the phone and spreadsheet they fall back on today, by moving the compliance and finance layers off their screens while keeping them one step away for the admins and executives who bought RouteLogic for them, because our largest account is deciding during its trial and coordinator usage is already falling across the base.

These are the core users. The cold read caught that my first draft hid this and read like a project to save one deal, so the hook and value proposition now say it outright. Still to prove: the exact workaround (phone, group text, spreadsheet) is my inference, and Module 2 research has to confirm it.
- **Does the value proposition actually remove the obstacle you identified, or is it a generic feature?:** It removes it, as long as the obstacle is the layers. The bar is now "faster than the phone and spreadsheet", not "fastest path in the product", because the fastest path inside a slow product can still lose to a phone. And the layers stay one step away for the executives who bought RouteLogic for them, so the buyer loses nothing. It would not help if the slowness is the software itself, such as load times, so I need a time baseline for assign and reroute before building.
