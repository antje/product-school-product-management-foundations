One stop, start to next, without the phone workaround

Strategy: strip & accelerate. Reclaim operational simplicity by stripping away legacy noise and ensuring the tool is the fastest for everyday work. Each bullet reads Action → Benefit.
Persona: a delivery driver making dozens of stops a day, often at a doorstep with one hand free.
Goal: confirm each stop and move on in seconds, with the rest of the route still there.
Today: three taps across three screens to mark a stop delivered, so the driver texts the dispatcher instead (UXR-01, BUG-2055).

User action

Internal state

Trusts the route is saved on the phone → no morning screenshot or paper copy.

Pain point addressed
